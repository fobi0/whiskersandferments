Since I am living in a small apartment and do not have a garden, it was always hard to get fresh herbs all year around. Indoor farming was an interesting idea and my hyperfixation for weeks got into this project.

I started with using just a glass with nutrient solution and tried to grew my first salad. This worked well, but the scale was just not enough. After some attempts I started to build a DWC (Deep Water Cultivation) system. This system is essentially just a big bucket of nutrient solution with some plants sitting in baskets feeding on that solution. The difference between my first attempts and this one is a pond-pump that pumps air into the buckets all day. This will provide enough oxigen for the plant roots (which they apparently need to survive).
For the first time in my live, I grew some plants from seedlings in small coco-pods and as soon as the roots where strong enough, I planted them in my mini garden. I also added growing lights above to help the plants grow. The lights are on 12h a day and the plants are thriving. As you can see on the picture I added, I am currently having some herbs and spring onions growing. The only thing I have to do is fill up the nutrient solution and cut the herbs. It really brings me a lot of joy (and food) and I am sure this will be a long time project with more and more improvements.


## What you will need:

- A bucket of some sort (for me it is small Euroboxes with approximately 10l of volume
- A pond pump ( a small one will be enough. make sure it is quiet enough for your living room)
- Hydroponic fertilizer
- Plants or Seeds that you grow to seedlings
- Hydroponic baskets
- Something that holds the Baskets in place ( I have a sheet of plastic with holes cut out)
- Growing lights ( I am using 100W LED growing lamps on half power)

Fill the bucket with the nutrient solution, add the plastic sheet on top and fill it with the baskets, add the pump into the water and turn it on. Add your plants into the baskets, install the lights above and wait. It will take a while till small seedlings really grow to harvest them, but it is worth it. Right now I could easily harvest 2-3 Times a week and I am using them for cooking all the time. The setup will surely be improved and updated soon, but it is exiting what you can grow without having a garden or a lot of space.

Enjoy
