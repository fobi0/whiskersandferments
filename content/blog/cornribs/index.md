It's corn! And it's also corn season! So let's make some barbecue ribs from it and enjoy them while summer slowly runs out. I love this recipe because it does what I want from a vegan adaptation of a dish: it gives the same feeling, the same fun, and the same mess while eating as the original, but it's still a unique and special dish that doesn't try to be meat but just stands on its own.

You can also smoke them over the fire or fry them, but this version is the easiest. This recipe is for 2 people with a lot of barbecue sauce left for dipping.

| Ingredient | Amount | Example | Step |
|------------|--------|---------|------|
| *For the barbecue sauce:* | | | |
| Tomato paste | 10% | 50g | |
| Soy Sauce | 3% | 15g | |
| Sugar | 5% | 25g | |
| Vinegar | 5% | 25g | |
| Liquid Smoke | 5% | 25g | |
| Rosemary, Paprika powder, Pepper, Garlic | to taste | | Mix all ingredients and heat them up in a pot. Let them simmer for 2-4 min and make sure everything is incorporated and the sauce is glossy. After that, pass it through a sieve and let it cool down. Adapt the consistency to your liking. |
| *For the Corn ribs:* | | | |
| Corn, raw | 100% | 500g | Use a heavy knife and split the corn cobs lengthwise into 4 pieces. |
| Oil | 10% | 50g | |
| Salt | 1% | 5g | Brush the corn ribs with salt in oil and bake them at 180°C for about 30 min. |
| Barbecue sauce | 15% | 75g | Brush the sauce on the corn ribs and grill them over high heat for another 10 min or until crispy and caramelized. Enjoy with more barbecue sauce. |
