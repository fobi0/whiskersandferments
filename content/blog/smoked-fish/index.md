Thrilled to be back after quite a while. Posting regularly is pretty hard for me. We are starting off with a new toy of mine for this year: A smoker. My journey to preserving food and making everything last longer lead me to buying a smoker and smoking fish for the first time in my live. And it was quite a success.

Smoking is a pretty interesting form of preserving. For longer preservation, you need to coldsmoke your produce for a long time. I will get to this, as soon as I am feeling ready to do this. For now, we are doing hot smoking. This will maybe make it last a little longer, but it is mainly for flavor.
## What you will need:

- Fish (I am using Makarel here)
- Salt
- Bay leaves
- Water
- A smoker

First you need to brine the fish. Create a brine with 10% Salt to Water ratio and put in the bay leaves. You can also add other aromatics if you want. Brine the fish in it for 12-24h.

Heat the smoker to 120°C and hang the fish inside and smoke it for 30-45min depending on the size of the fish. The backfin of the fish should be easily removable by hand, when it is ready.

After that serve it right away or let it cool down and eat in later. The smoke improves the flavorprofile by a lot and makes it super interesting to work with.

I already did 2 more rounds of smoking fish with different fishes and wood. I am very pleased with the results, even though it took a while to get to know the smoker. I am exited to do more smoking experiments in the future.

Enjoy
