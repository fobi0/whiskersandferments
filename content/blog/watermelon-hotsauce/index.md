Today we are doing an all time favourites of mine: Hotsauce. I regularly do Hotsauces of all kind, store them in my fridge and put them on everything, I want to have a little heat with.

Hotsauces in general are pretty simple. You get your Chilis, Salt and then work from there. It usually helps to have some kind of Sweetness with it.. for this recipe, I have choosen Watermelon. Not fermented Hotsauces usually need some acidity to them, but fermenting creates the acidity with the lactofermentation by itself.

## What you will need:

- 200g Chilis of your choice (I have choosen peperoni chili because they were available at this time)
- 200g Watermelon
- 2 Cloves of Garlic
- Water
- Salt

Take all ingredients and cut them into smaller chunks. Put it in a jar and cover it with water. Measure the weight of the ingredients (including the water added) and add 2% of salt to the mix.

Leave it at room temperature, waiting for the fermentation to do its magic. This usually takes a week, till the fermentation reached a PH Level of 3-4. Depending on the room temperature and the lactobacillus available this can take shorter or longer.

After that, blend the ingredients till smooth. I would start with a little bit of the water first and slowly blend in the water, till the consistency is reached. The remaining water can be used for dressings or as shot.

This recipe works with most ingredients and can be widely adapted to your likings.

Enjoy
