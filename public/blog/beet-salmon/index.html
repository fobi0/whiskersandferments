Smoked yellow beet 'salmon'

10.10.2023

We already had beetroot sashimi in an earlier post. This time we are using yellow beets and marinating them a little differently. This is not a replacement for classic smoked salmon, but it gives a perfect alternative that stands on its own, without imitating the flavor of fish, but giving a smoky, filling, and absolutely delicious option for salads, sandwiches, bagels, or the next vegan brunch.

Ingredient Amount Example Step
Yellow beet 100% 500g Brush some oil on it and bake it in the oven for around 45 min at 180°C. The inside needs to be soft and a fork should go in without much resistance.
For the brine:
Soy Sauce 20% 100g
Water 40% 200g
Salt 2% 10g
Pepper 1% 5g
Rice Vinegar 20% 100g
Sugar 2% 10g
Paprika Powder 0.5% 2.5g
Vegetable oil 4% 20g
Mix all the ingredients until the sugar and salt are dissolved. We are making a kind of vinaigrette here.
Cut the beets into thin slices. We will not dry them this time, so you can make them as thin as you want them later in the process.
Add the pieces to a jar and pour the brine over them. Make sure every beet is covered with the brine. Leave it in the fridge for at least 2 days. It will stay fresh for multiple days to a week afterwards.
After the curing process, take them out of the liquid and put them in a box for smoking. I use cold smoking with a smoking gun. It’s easier and I can do it on the balcony of my apartment without creating a fire hazard. After that, put them back in the fridge and keep them covered so they don’t dry out too much.
When you want to serve them, take them out of the fridge and brush each slice with some oil. Serve them cool and enjoy this treat :)
