Biang Biang Noodles

08.22.2023

This recipe was always something I wanted to try, but it looks so complex to hand-pull noodles. I imagined ending up with a sticky ball of strings that you can chew on, but it is surprisingly easy. The important part is to let it rest for a few hours. Good thing: it lasts at least 3-4 days in the fridge, so you can make the dough way ahead, and the actual time to serve with ready dough is 15-20 min. The oil, garlic, and chili make it perfect comfort food for the evening. You can fry some meats, tofu, or egg and just add it to the dish.

The measurements are based on the amount of dough you are making. For 2 servings, I recommend using 150g-200g with 50% hydration by weight.

Ingredient Amount Example Step
For the dough:
Flour 100% 200g
Water 50% 100g
Salt 1% 2g
Mix all the ingredients until smooth. Let them sit covered for 45 min. Then knead it again and shape it into a rectangle form, around 2 cm thick. Cut it into 3-4 cm pieces and brush some oil on them so that they don’t stick. Then leave it in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
For the toppings:
Garlic 3% 6g Finely chopped
Chiliflakes 2% 4g
Ginger 1% 2g Finely chopped
Cumin 1% 2g
Szechuan Peppercorns 1% 2g
Vegetable Oil 30% 60g Heat up
When ready, set up some salted boiling water. As soon as it boils, you can start. Make an imprint of a chopstick horizontally across the middle of the longer side of each dough package. This imprint will help to divide the noodles in the middle later. Take one of the dough packages and hold it with your index finger and thumb on each side. Slowly pull them and slap the middle on the table to pull it into a long noodle string. Divide the string in the middle to make a giant noodle loop out of it. Immediately boil it for 2-3 min and place it in a bowl. Repeat for all packages.
Top the noodles with your toppings and make sure that you pile them up in the middle. Pour over the hot oil. It will be infused by the toppings and get a delicious flavor.
For Decorations:
Spring onion 10% 20g
Sesame seeds 5% 10g
For the Sauce:
Soy Sauce 30% 60g
Chinese Malt Vinegar 20% 40g
