Vegan Bolognese

08.22.2023

We are starting with one of my all-time favorites: vegan bolognese. It is not comparable to an actual bolognese, but it resembles many of the textures and flavors quite well and is a good, quick, and easy option for making spaghetti or lasagna. It’s also much quicker than actual bolognese since there’s no meat to add to the flavor profile. Soy sauce and red wine, together with mushrooms, still add a lot of depth to the dish and help give you the umami flavor needed for a good bolognese. I use the same base for chili sin carne as well, by adding cacao, beans, chilis, and some more spices.

For two people, I recommend around 150-200g of mushrooms to start. The recipe is based on the weight of the mushrooms, so if you need food for more people, just multiply the amount of mushrooms by the number of servings, then multiply its weight by the percentages. For example, if you have 4 people, that’s 400g of mushrooms, so you would need 400g * 0.3 = 120g of tofu.

Ingredient Amount Example Step
For the “Meat”:
Button Mushrooms 100% 200g Grate them roughly.
Tofu 60% 120g Crush them with your hands into fine pieces.
Carrots 60% 120g Grate them roughly.
Vegetable oil 20% 40g
Fry all the above ingredients in oil for at least 5 minutes. They should develop a crust and a lot of flavor.
Soy Sauce 10% 20g Pour it over the fried mix.
For the Sauce:
Celery 40% 80g Dice.
Onion 40% 80g Dice.
Carrots 40% 80g Dice.
Vegetable oil 10% 20g Fry the three ingredients in a new pot to get the amazing soffritto as a base for this dish. Make sure everything is cooked well (should take 5-7 min).
Garlic 5% 10g Grate finely and add to the pot.
Oregano 2% 4g Add it to the pot together with the “meat mixture” from before and stir.
Red wine 100% 200g Pour over the wine and let it simmer for a few minutes.
Canned Tomatoes 200% 400g Add tomatoes and let it simmer for at least 30 min (better would be 45).
Tomato Paste 10% 20g Thicken it up with tomato paste.
Salt and Pepper To taste Add salt and pepper to taste.
