Pork Belly Radish Skewers

08.24.2023

I am a big fan of skewers. They’re easy to make, fun to eat, and almost every food culture in the world has some kind of skewer dish. This combination is one of my favorites. The fat of the pork belly combined with the crunchy radish and the gochujang is just too tasty not to try.

You can either grill them over direct heat or put them in the oven on broil mode. It is important to have high heat to get a little bit of charring on them.

Ingredient Amount Example Step
For the Marinade:
Gochujang 100% 15g
Soy Sauce 100% 15g
Mix them together to form a thick paste.
For the skewers:
Pork belly 100% 150g Remove the skin and cut the pork belly into small pieces.
Radish 100% 150g Cut them in half.
Add the pork belly and radish to a bowl and add the marinade. Let it sit for at least 2 hours.
Put them on skewers and roast them on a grill for around 10 min, turning them every minute or so. Make sure they have a nice char and serve them.
