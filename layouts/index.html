Welcome to my cozy corner of the internet!
This is where fermentation, flavor, and occasional
other hyperfixations of mine get posted.
I am passionate about everything food and drink related.
In the past, I have successfully made Koji, various ferments, I owned a Kombucha, kept my sourdough starter alive for 7 years now and focus on old and new techniques of cooking.
For me, cooking is relaxing, exiting, and fascinating at once. I jump into every rabbithole I can find and testing out various things.
Canning, Fermenting, Drying and other preserving options are something I just do for fun. Working with local ingredients and going with the seasons became a part of my daily live, that I don't want to miss anymore.
I am not a professional or anything, but I am having a ton of fun and want to share the things I have learned for friends, family, others and also myself to remember.
Enjoy your stay!
